About Us

The Healing Hands Behind Mayo's Calm

One therapist, fifteen years of mastery, and a quiet studio on the road between Newport and Mulranny.

Meet the Founder

Chiraphat Kakaeo

Born and raised in Thailand, Chiraphat trained at the world-renowned Wat Po Traditional Medical School in Bangkok — the temple school where Thai massage itself was first codified. After qualifying in 2011, she spent years refining her craft before making the west of Ireland her home.

In 2022, she founded Mayo Authentic Thai Massage in her studio at Knockbreaga, bringing genuine, temple-trained Thai therapy to Co. Mayo. Her clients — over a thousand of them, and counting — know her for strong, intuitive hands and an unhurried, deeply caring approach.

  • Certificate of Achievement, Thai Traditional Massage — The Wat Po Traditional Medical School, Bangkok (2011)
  • Registered Irish Business — Mayo Authentic Thai Massage, est. August 2022
  • 15 years of professional practice in traditional Thai and therapeutic massage
