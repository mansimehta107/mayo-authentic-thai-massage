About This Massage

Ancient Healing, Restored

Traditional Thai massage is the signature therapy of our studio, performed exactly as it is taught at the Wat Po Traditional Medical School in Bangkok. You stay fully clothed in loose, comfortable wear while the therapist works along the body's energy lines using palms, thumbs, elbows and forearms.

Rhythmic acupressure is combined with gentle, assisted yoga-like stretches that open the joints and lengthen the muscles. Performed on a padded floor mat, it is deeply restorative yet energising — most people leave feeling lighter, looser and more mobile.
