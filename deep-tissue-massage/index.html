About This Massage

Firm Pressure, Lasting Relief

Deep tissue massage uses slow, deliberate strokes and sustained pressure to reach the deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue. It is a more intensive treatment, ideal for chronic tension, old injuries and areas that simply won't let go.

Your therapist works gradually, warming the tissue before applying focused pressure to release adhesions and knots. You may feel firm sensation during the work, but the result is a profound, lasting release.
