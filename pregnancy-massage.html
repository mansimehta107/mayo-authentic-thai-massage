About This Massage

Gentle Care for Two

Pregnancy massage is a specialised, gentle treatment designed for expectant mothers. Using a comfortable side-lying position with supportive cushions, it safely relieves the aches and strains that come with a changing body.

Chiraphat has supported many mothers across Mayo through pregnancy. Pressure is always light to moderate, areas are carefully chosen, and your comfort guides every moment of the session. Recommended after the first trimester.
