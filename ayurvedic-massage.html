About This Massage

An Ancient Ritual

Inspired by the Indian tradition of Abhyanga, Ayurvedic massage uses generous amounts of warm herbal oil and long, rhythmic strokes worked toward the heart. It is a deeply grounding, full-body ritual.

The treatment calms the nervous system, supports lymphatic flow and circulation, and deeply nourishes dry skin. It is wonderfully restorative for those feeling depleted, anxious or simply in need of deep rest.
