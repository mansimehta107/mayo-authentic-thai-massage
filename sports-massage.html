About This Massage

For Bodies in Motion

Sports massage is a firm, targeted treatment for active people — runners, GAA players, swimmers and gym-goers. It flushes out tight muscles, addresses overused areas and helps the body recover between training.

Beyond recovery, regular sports massage helps prevent injury by keeping muscles supple and balanced. Pressure is firm and focused on the areas your activity loads most.
